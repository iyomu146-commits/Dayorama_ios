Current sky renderer (2026-09-20)

Milky Way: original procedural graphics, with no source image or image asset.
The luminous galactic band, dust lanes and 110,000 faint particles are
illustrative. They are not a one-to-one reconstruction of observed nebulae
or catalog stars. Their shape is deterministic and fixed on the sky sphere.
The diffuse layer is shaded on the GPU and cached in a transient render
target until the camera, observation date/site or viewport changes.

Celestial/Galactic reference-frame conversion:
ESA Gaia EDR3 documentation, section 4.1.7, equation 4.62.
https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/documentation/GEDR3/Data_processing/chap_cu3ast/sec_cu3ast_intro/ssec_cu3ast_intro_tansforms.html

Actual foreground stars, constellation figures and names:
d3-celestial, Copyright (c) 2015 Olaf Frohn. BSD-3-Clause.
https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial
See LICENSE-d3-celestial.txt (bundled in full).
stars.6.json: 5,044 catalog stars; RA, declination, magnitude, B-V color index.

The earlier NASA-derived milkyway-4k.webp and its CREDITS.txt remain in the
development sources for provenance. The current renderer does not load
them, and the native application does not bundle them.
