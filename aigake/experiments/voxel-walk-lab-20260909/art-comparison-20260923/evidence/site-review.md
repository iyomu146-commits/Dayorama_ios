# Storefront and site pass

The prior 175,366-cell craft version remains selectable. The current third version is labelled 窓と敷地. It changes composition as well as geometry, so it must not be presented as a pure renderer A/B against the unchanged modular control.

Changes: genuinely recessed opaque glazing, inner perimeter beads with no central mullion, continuous window reveals, deeper sill and two small real potted plants. The pane's diagonal reflection is authored cell colour, not a photograph or an interior projection; no real-time reflected scene is claimed. The cafe interior is still not modelled.

The former thick oval mound and repetitive paving grid are replaced with a flat polygonal site, earth edge, varied chamfered flagstones, doorway approach, terrace paving and three planting patches. Footings are preserved beneath the building and furniture. Validation found the inherited doorway planter encroaching on the circulation area; its centre moved from x=1.48m to x=1.84m together with its flowers and support stone.

The first open-canopy version looked too sparse and clipped into flat crowns. The correction increased vertical spread and clusters to 38 per crown, with overlapping leaf fans and quieter olive colours. Gaps still expose major branches. The tree is a connected voxel form; botanical or physical simulation is not claimed.

Visual checks use the running browser at the same camera/light/grid settings. Whole-scene and reverse views inspect site attachment; close views inspect the window and furniture. Image-fidelity acceptance against the generated reference remains incomplete. This is a stylized editable scene study, not an automatic photogrammetry result.

Tests: 12 existing tests plus 5 scene tests cover clear window recesses, preserved glass, foundation/furniture/pot support, entrance clearance, tree connectivity, roof coverage, temporary framing and both palette/phase file round-trips. Phase sorting prevents repeated twig/leaf overlaps from accumulating redundant build layers in exported files.

Completed scene: 144,042 final occupied cells and 211,956 visible surface triangles, compared with the prior 175,366 cells and 213,088 triangles. Counts include ground and all props, including hidden solid cells; temporary underlayers are not added to the final occupied-cell total. Lower cell count alone is not a device-performance measurement. iPhone FPS, memory and heat are still unmeasured.
