Edit the supplied WHOLE-TOWN VOXEL reference. PRIMARY CORRECTION: the walls and roofs look too uniformly clean and flat. Make their actual voxel colours and relief visibly authored, varied, tactile, lived-in, while keeping a calm, carefully maintained town.
WALLS: substantial readable clustered colour variation, not random per-pixel noise. In the house's ivory upper storey use uneven warm cream / muted pale peach / cool gray-beige plaster patches, placed in connected groups of 2–5 cubes and a few narrow repair bands. Lower sage walls have pale yellow-green exposed plaster, restrained blue-green shadowed patches and a slightly earthier plinth. Give the bakery terracotta masonry alternating clay, muted burnt pink, warm ochre and some dark purplish brown bricks in a credible staggered bond; joints and the edges of lintels have real shallow voxel relief. Bookshop indigo paint has dusty blue, muted violet-blue and desaturated teal patches, a faded lower strip and warmer stone lintels. Flower shop ochre plaster has apricot and pale buff repair patches with cooler brown-beige recesses. Cafe ivory plaster has warm pink-beige grouped patches, cool gray-beige wall-return areas and a varied burnt-clay brick plinth. Corner quoins, window reveals and footing courses should have distinct depths. Patches should remain readable at the whole-town size, not microscopic mottling. Aim for 3–5 deliberately coordinated tones per surface.
ROOFS: make each stepped roof a visibly assembled set of chunky tile/copper/painted-metal courses; mix nearby hues within bounded tile groups and bands. Foliage: asymmetric clustered value/hue variation, preserving visible branches and chunky grid size.
Light: preserve warm side sunlight + cool sky fill but strengthen restrained contact shadows under roof eaves, within recessed windows and beneath furniture. Hue differences belong to actual blocks and remain visible under neutral lighting; do not fake all texture with a dramatic grading filter. Calm, rich colour, matte plaster, satin roof, wood, stone and muted reflective glazing each read differently. Keep broad readable quiet areas between accents.

No words, text, UI, labels, logos or oversized decorative objects. Same camera and entire composition, no cropping. This is a refined engine-reproducible COARSE voxel game art target.
