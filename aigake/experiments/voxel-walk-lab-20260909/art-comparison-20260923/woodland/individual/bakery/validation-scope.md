# Geometry validation scope

The generic `self_intersection.py` vertex-ray probe reports inside vertices on this axis-aligned union. The original blockout report and both alternate tessellation/normal experiments are retained. None is a pass. Averaging normals and replacing greedy faces with equivalent unit faces did not resolve the diagnostic, so those attempts were abandoned.

For this model, `check-boundary.mjs` checks the **actual assembled browser mesh** against the authoritative, unique integer-cell occupancy. Every vertex must be on the 15cm grid; every triangle must lie in an axis plane with the outward sign; the complete signed unit-face set must equal the occupancy boundary, and summed triangle area must equal boundary area. This establishes absence of hidden interior faces, missing boundary area, duplicated surface area, or geometry outside the voxel boundary. It is a domain-specific replacement for the vertex-ray heuristic, not a claim that the generic script passed. Runtime tests separately check support, passages, glazing, and roof coverage.

The shadow receiver is excluded from object geometry and silhouette evidence. Individual parts intentionally share contact boundaries; exploded meshes regenerate those normally hidden boundaries. A single reference does not reveal the back/interior; those are conservative inferred closures.

Turntable permits silhouette-enclosed background: inspected side views show the intentional space between shop facade, canopy and the two freestanding bread bins, plus space between the bin legs. This is not a solid-volume subject. Roof closure and support are independently asserted from occupancy. The initial structure diagnostic (.7999 IoU) failed after an over-wide left planter changed camera framing; moving the planter closer restored the silhouette. The source-side concept measurements in the spec are estimates, not final bounds; runtime cell bounds are authoritative.

The generic component checker excludes parentless nodes with children as presumed abstract roots. In this model, the shop, two bins and two pots are real geometry as well as logical roots. They are therefore passed explicitly through `--require building,pot-left,pot-right,stall-left,stall-right`. `runtime.test.mjs` also asserts exact equality of the actual 27 part names and the 27 authored cell-part names. Informational root-exclusion messages do not mean those objects were unspecified or unbuilt.

The early pass checklists were not marked at the time, although actual renders, diagnostics and ordered reviews exist. Their archived pending entries are retained as an audit limitation; lighting onward records checklist steps through the state tool. Do not reinterpret this as every original checklist having been completed in sequence. Final state reflects the existing evidence and current delivery checks.
